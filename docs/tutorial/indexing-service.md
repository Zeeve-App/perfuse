# Using the Traceye Platform for Subgraphs

## Purchase Subscription

Follow these steps to purchase a subscription:

### Step 1: Register/Login
Create a new account or log in to an existing account on the Traceye Platform.

### Step 2: Navigate to the Purchase Page
- Go to the **Hosted SubQuery** purchase page by clicking on **Hosted SubQuery** above the **SHARED INDEXING** section in the left navigation pane.
- Click on the **Buy Subscription** button at the top.

### Step 3: Enter Quantity
- Provide the number of Subqueries, additional API units, and additional storage units you want to purchase.
- Click on the **Buy Now** button to proceed.

![Step 3: Enter Quantity](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/15ef985b-a909-44ca-af35-7ed22cf5b905)

### Step 4: Make Payment
- On the payment page, enter your card details.
- Click on **Subscribe** to complete the payment.

![Step 4: Make Payment](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ef301b0e-ad2d-43ab-be0a-fce632a0a0c3)

🎉 **Congratulations!** You have successfully purchased your subscription.

## Manage Hosted SubQuery

### Viewing and Creating SubQueries
- View a list of all existing subqueries and create new ones using the **Add SubQuery** button.
- Click on a subQuery to view its details, including:
  - Blockchain Family
  - Blockchain Network
  - RPC Endpoint
  - Creation Date
- SubQuery details also display the **Deployment Command** and the **Query URL**.

### Monitoring SubQuery Status
The status of a subQuery is displayed on its detail page and can include the following:
- **Not deployed**: SubQuery created but not deployed.
- **In Progress**: SubQuery is being deployed.
- **Failed**: SubQuery deployment failed.
- **Deployed**: SubQuery is deployed and syncing data.

## Work with SubQuery

### Step 1: Register/Login
Create a new account or log in to an existing account on the Traceye Platform.

### Step 2: Navigate to Hosted SubQuery
Click on **Hosted SubQuery** above the **SHARED INDEXING** section in the left navigation panel.

### Step 3: SubQuery Dashboard
You will be redirected to the **SubQuery Dashboard** screen to view a list of all existing subqueries.

![Step 3: SubQuery Dashboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3685132e-9e19-4827-963e-b31b885f8e33)

### Step 4: Add a SubQuery
Click on the **Add SubQuery** button at the top right of the screen. You will be redirected to the **Create SubQuery** screen.

![Step 4: Add a SubQuery](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9cf7becd-219b-4564-a53f-d95e0f3e2fe3)

### Step 5: Enter SubQuery Details
- Provide valid details for the subQuery.
- Click on the **Add SubQuery** button. Upon validation, the subQuery is created, and you are redirected to its detail page.

![Step 5: Enter SubQuery Details](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/48a662eb-eff8-44fb-a466-9ed9a56b11da)

### Step 6: View SubQuery Details
The details page includes:
- Blockchain Family
- Blockchain Network
- RPC Endpoint (parachain endpoint deployed by Perfuse)
- Creation Date
- API units consumed
- Request metadata
- SubQuery Query URL
- SubQuery Deployment Command

![Step 6: View SubQuery Details](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b311b579-fef4-4957-a828-3b8637dbbd2e)

### Step 7: Deployment and Sync
- Once deployed, the status changes from **Not deployed** to **Deployed**.
- The subQuery starts indexing, showing **Sync Status**, **Request Data**, and **Query URL**.

![Step 7: Deployment and Sync](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5a2463b3-98f7-49cd-bf1b-9c331d734b48)

### Step 8: Query URL
The **Query URL** section provides the URL for making GraphQL queries. Click the button to access the **GraphQL Playground**.

![Step 8: Query URL](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3c5603ae-1181-4311-8cb9-16f87bcf5e50)  
![GraphQL Playground](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5b0f310d-e4c4-4218-857e-e0de2b6877f7)

### Step 9: View Logs
Click on the **Log** button of any deployed subQuery to monitor real-time sync status.

![Step 9: View Logs](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/92e2e05d-759d-4392-a4dc-8377c70119f4)

### Step 10: View Entities
Click on the **Entities** tab of a deployed subQuery to view its associated entities.

![Step 10: View Entities](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/39441c75-5e92-4e59-a7cc-a42b5f7486d8)

### Step 11: Delete a SubQuery
- Click on the **Delete** button to remove a subQuery.
- Confirm the deletion to return to the **Hosted SubQuery** dashboard.

![Step 11: Delete a SubQuery](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4554e0fd-62de-4930-92b8-062555343dfe)
